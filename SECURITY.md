# Security policy

Report suspected vulnerabilities privately to
[security@delzory.com](mailto:security@delzory.com).

This policy covers the source in this repository and the public status page at
[status.delzory.com](https://status.delzory.com).

## Reporting a vulnerability

Include a concise description, potential impact, the affected URL or file, and
minimal reproduction steps using accounts and data you control. Remove passwords,
tokens, personal information, and unrelated customer data from your report.

Do not disclose vulnerabilities, exploit details, credentials, or customer
information in public pull requests, comments, or incident posts. Allow time for
investigation and coordinated disclosure.

This policy does not authorize disruptive testing or access to other people's
accounts or data. No response deadline or payment is promised.

For general service or account assistance, use your existing Delzory support
channel.
