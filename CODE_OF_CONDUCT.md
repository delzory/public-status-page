# Code of conduct

Delzory expects respectful, professional participation in this repository and
related project communications. These expectations apply to maintainers,
contributors, and anyone taking part in discussions.

## Respect people and their work

- Discuss ideas and technical decisions constructively.
- Be considerate of different backgrounds, identities, abilities, and experience.
- Accept feedback and take responsibility for mistakes.
- Protect other people's privacy and confidential information.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, sexualized conduct, personal attacks,
and publishing private information without permission are unacceptable. Do not
use repository discussions or direct messages to intimidate or repeatedly target
another participant.

## Report a concern privately

Contact [contact@dev.delzory.com](mailto:contact@dev.delzory.com) with a description
of the behavior, relevant links or sanitized evidence, and a way to follow up.
Avoid posting sensitive reports publicly. Security vulnerabilities should instead
follow the [security policy](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers will assess reports and choose a response appropriate to the behavior
and its impact. Responses may include a request to correct behavior, removal of
content, or restrictions on participation. Report details should be shared only
as needed to assess and address the concern.

## Scope and attribution

These standards apply in project spaces and when someone represents Delzory in
connection with this project.
